What types of work do you take on?

Galleys covers building and landscaping. Work shared on our Facebook page includes fencing, gates, shed bases and ground preparation for lawns. Get in touch to discuss the scope of your particular project.

Where are you based?

We’re based in Chelmsford, Essex. Send Lee your postcode and a short description of the work to check availability in your area.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call 07939 836799, send a WhatsApp message or email galley1981@hotmail.com. Include your location and what you’d like to do, and Lee can discuss the next steps with you.

Can I send photos or plans of my project?

Yes. You can share photos and plans by WhatsApp or email. Approximate dimensions and a description of the result you have in mind will also help the conversation.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

You can see project updates on our Facebook page. Explore our project galleries for photographs of our building and landscaping work, including kitchens, conservatories, decking and garden improvements.

When could my project start?

Timing depends on the type of work and current availability. Contact Lee with your preferred dates to discuss a suitable schedule.
